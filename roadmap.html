FUTURE ROADMAP

Plan Your Future

Career planning is a process. Explore, learn, build skills and make informed decisions.

01 — 🔎 Explore Yourself

Understand your interests, strengths, favorite activities and personal goals.

Use the FutureGuide questionnaire to discover possible career directions.

02 — 📚 Choose Subjects

Compare subject combinations such as PCM, PCB, Commerce and Humanities.

Think about which subjects you enjoy and which careers they can lead toward.

03 — 🧠 Build Skills

Develop useful skills alongside your school studies.

Examples include communication, problem solving, digital skills, teamwork and creativity.

04 — 🎓 Higher Education

Research courses, colleges, entrance requirements and possible education pathways.

05 — 🚀 Career Preparation

Gain experience through projects, competitions, internships, volunteering or other practical activities.

06 — 🌟 Keep Growing

Your first career choice does not have to determine your entire life.

Keep learning, adapting and exploring new opportunities.

💡 Remember

FutureGuide provides suggestions to help you explore possibilities. Your interests, circumstances and goals can change over time.
