Career Options

Explore different career paths and understand what each one involves.

💻 Software Engineer

Designs, develops and maintains websites, applications and software.

Useful skills: Programming, logic, problem solving and mathematics.

📊 Data Scientist

Uses data, statistics and technology to discover patterns and solve problems.

Useful skills: Mathematics, statistics, programming and analytical thinking.

🩺 Doctor

Diagnoses illnesses, treats patients and works in healthcare.

Useful skills: Science, communication, patience and decision-making.

📈 Chartered Accountant

Works with accounting, finance, taxation and business information.

Useful skills: Mathematics, attention to detail, analysis and organisation.

⚖️ Lawyer

Works with laws, legal research, arguments and legal services.

Useful skills: Communication, reasoning, research and confidence.

🎨 UI/UX Designer

Designs digital interfaces that are attractive, useful and easy to use.

Useful skills: Creativity, design, communication and understanding users.
