Subject Combinations

Explore different subject combinations and the career paths they can lead toward.

🧮 PCM

Physics + Chemistry + Mathematics

A strong option for students interested in engineering, technology, mathematics, architecture and related fields.

Possible careers: Software Engineer, Engineer, Data Scientist, Architect.

🧬 PCB

Physics + Chemistry + Biology

Suitable for students interested in medicine, healthcare, biology and life sciences.

Possible careers: Doctor, Dentist, Pharmacist, Biologist and healthcare careers.

💼 Commerce

Accountancy + Business Studies + Economics

A useful pathway for students interested in finance, business, accounting, economics and entrepreneurship.

Possible careers: Chartered Accountant, Finance Professional, Entrepreneur and Business Manager.

📖 Humanities

Humanities and Social Sciences

Suitable for students interested in law, civil services, psychology, society, history, media and communication.

Possible careers: Lawyer, Civil Services, Teacher, Writer and Journalist.
