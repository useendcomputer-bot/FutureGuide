About FutureGuide

What is FutureGuide?

FutureGuide is a student-focused career and subject guidance platform designed to help students explore possible directions for their future.

Students answer questions about their interests, activities, strengths and goals. FutureGuide then uses a points-based system to suggest careers and subject combinations that may match their answers.

🎯 Our Goal

The goal of FutureGuide is to make career exploration simple, interactive and easier for students to understand.

💻 How It Works

1. Answer the questionnaire.

2. Your answers are converted into points.

3. Career and subject scores are calculated.

4. Your highest matches are displayed.

⚠️ Important Note

FutureGuide is an educational project created for career exploration. Its recommendations are suggestions and should not be treated as a professional career assessment.
